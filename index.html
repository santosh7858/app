2 Mode, dance aur speed

✏️ 2D Stick-man settings

Haath se bane stick-man dance / jhagra / Badshah video. Characters ki ginti aur type neeche se chuno (sab stick-man hi bante hain). Background, stage, lights, camera aur kapde sirf 3D ke liye hain.

VS mode me Auto teams par 1 fighter baaki sabhi ke khilaaf ladega. Woh opponents ko ek-ek karke haraayega; aakhri opponent ke baad 3 second ka attitude/funk pose aayega. Manual teams chuno to apni teams bana sakte ho.

Words tabhi dikhenge jab neeche "Video par text" ON ho. Bheed sirf tab dikhti hai jab "Public" ON ho.

Har character ka type chuno (Badshah, Ninja, Ladki, Pehelwan...) ya Random chodo. Dance style har ek ke alag random aate hain.

Blur lagane par video banne me zyada time lagta hai (normal ~4x, strong ~6x).

Kuch nahi chuna to upar wali theme ka background chalega. "3D Stage Room" theme me 3D jaisa kamra banta hai, "Paper Flipbook" me page palatte hain aur page number likha hota hai.

⚔️ Fight settings

Kam se kam 2 dancers chahiye (kam hue to apne aap add honge). 3D VS me dono teams daudkar aati hain. 2D VS Auto me ek fighter baaki sabhi se ek-ek karke ladega. Talwar / sword / axe se sequential head/hand cuts dikh sakte hain; aakhri opponent ke girne ke baad 3 second attitude/funk pose aayega.
